Sweet Spot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Sweet Spot has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It never connects to the internet, so nothing you do in it is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress on each puzzle, and whether you’ve seen How to play, are saved in the app on your device. “Start over” clears a puzzle. Deleting the app clears everything.

Sharing

Share and Copy hand your result, the puzzle number and your ● ◐ ○ marks, to the share sheet or clipboard only when you tap them. Where it goes from there is up to you.

Nothing else

  • No personal information is collected, stored on a server, or shared.
  • No third-party SDKs, advertising, or analytics.
  • No device permissions: no contacts, location, photos, camera, or microphone.

Children

Sweet Spot collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new date above.
