Venn

Seven words. Three mystery circles. Figure out where each one belongs.

Each puzzle hides three categories, drawn as overlapping circles. Drag each word into one of the seven spaces, submit, and use the feedback to work out the categories. You get six tries.

Getting help

Email noah@nsgsolutions.co. Useful things to include: the puzzle number, what you were doing, and your iPhone or iPad model.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Venn has no accounts and works offline.
  • A puzzle has an answer I disagree with. Please tell us the puzzle number and the word. We’ll look at it and fix it in an update if it’s wrong.
  • How do I replay a puzzle? Open it, tap the … menu, and choose “Start over”.
  • Can I play older puzzles? Yes. Every puzzle is in the grid on the home screen.

Privacy
